## 1 Checks Before Installation

Unpack the unit and check the contents against the packing list: one unit, one mounting bracket set, one grid cable, one communication antenna, a quick guide and a warranty card. If the enclosure is dented, a connector is distorted or an accessory is missing, raise a ticket with your installer within twenty-four hours and attach photographs. Do not power the unit up yourself.

Installation must be carried out by a licensed installer. Before starting, confirm that the consumer unit has a spare circuit-breaker position and that the voltage at the grid connection point is within the range printed on the nameplate. Damage caused by owner-installed equipment is not covered by the warranty.

Check that the product serial number matches on the shipping box, on the nameplate and in the after-sales system before mounting. If the three do not match, stop the installation and contact WOTAI Energy after-sales.

## 2 Mounting Location and Clearances

The unit may be installed indoors or on a sheltered outdoor wall. Avoid direct sunlight and direct rain. The mounting surface must carry the full weight of the unit over its service life: concrete and solid brick walls can take the bracket directly, while stud walls need a backing plate.

Leave service clearances around the unit: at least thirty centimetres above, thirty centimetres below and twenty centimetres to each side. Do not install the unit above a gas meter, above flammable material or within reach of children, and do not block an existing fire escape route.

Keep the ambient temperature between minus twenty and plus fifty-five degrees Celsius. Charging is blocked below minus twenty degrees Celsius; above forty-five degrees Celsius the unit reduces output automatically.

## 3 Electrical Connection

The grid connection point must be single-phase 230 V with a tolerance of plus or minus ten percent, that is 207 V to 253 V, at 50 Hz with a tolerance of plus or minus five percent, that is 47.5 Hz to 51.5 Hz. Three-phase models require 400 V with the same ten percent tolerance.

Wire the grid cable by colour: brown to line, blue to neutral, yellow-green to earth. Earth resistance must be below four ohms; the unit must not be connected to the grid without a reliable earth. Install a circuit breaker and a surge protection device on the AC side that match the rated current of the unit.

Use the supplied DC cable between the battery and the inverter. If it is too short, extend it with the cross-section specified in the manual; never use a smaller cross-section. Measure the DC polarity before closing the switch: reversed polarity destroys the inverter immediately and is not covered by the warranty.

## 4 Commissioning and Grid Registration

Close the battery switch first and wait for the indicator to stay lit, then close the AC circuit breaker. Self-test takes about two minutes and passes when the indicator turns solid green, which means the unit is feeding the grid.

Connect your phone to the unit hotspot and enter the voltage at the grid connection point, the grid-connection date and the installer number on the commissioning page. The unit then uploads the registration record to the after-sales system, and the warranty start date is taken from that record.

If the unit shows a fault code, look it up in the fault code table and follow the steps given there. When the fault requires a reset, hold the reset key for five seconds and wait for the self-test to finish. If the same fault code returns after three resets, stop and raise a ticket.

## 5 Common Installation Issues

High voltage at the grid connection point is the most frequent reason for a refused grid connection, typically in areas with dense photovoltaic penetration. Measure the open-circuit voltage first; above 253 V, ask the grid operator to adjust the transformer tap rather than bypassing the protection in the unit settings.

When the unit reports low insulation resistance, disconnect the DC side and measure each string to earth with an insulation tester. Look for strings below one megaohm and check damp connectors. Do not keep resetting the unit before the cause is found.

Communication loss is usually caused by a badly placed antenna or a weak router signal. Move the antenna to the outward-facing side or switch to wired Ethernet on the commissioning page; historical data is uploaded automatically once the link is restored.
