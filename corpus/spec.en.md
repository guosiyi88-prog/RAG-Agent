## 1 Balcony Storage ESS-600

Balcony storage is aimed at apartments and rented housing. The set consists of two three-hundred-watt monocrystalline modules and a built-in micro-inverter, mounted on a balcony railing or an outer wall and connected to the grid through a household socket. The owner can fix the bracket and plug the unit in, but the grid-connection registration must still be submitted by a licensed installer in the after-sales system, otherwise the warranty start date cannot be established. ## 2 Residential Storage ESS-5000

Residential storage is aimed at detached houses. A hybrid inverter and a lithium iron phosphate battery store surplus photovoltaic energy during the day, discharge at night, and switch to backup output when the grid fails. The unit is mounted on a wall near the consumer unit, with the battery on the DC side and the grid connection point on the AC side; backup loads must be wired to the dedicated backup circuit. ## 3 Commercial and Industrial Storage ESS-C100

Commercial storage serves factories, business parks and charging sites. It combines a liquid-cooled battery cabinet with a separate power conversion system, supports parallel cabinets for expansion, and delivers one hundred kilowatts with a capacity of two hundred and fifteen kilowatt-hours per cabinet. The parallel layout is fixed at project design stage; the AC combiner cabinet and the monitoring gateway are supplied per project list, and site work and acceptance are carried out by a contractor holding the required qualification. ## 4 Packing Contents

The balcony storage set contains one micro-inverter WT-MI600, two three-hundred-watt modules, a railing bracket set, a grid cable with plug and a quick guide. The extension battery is optional and must be ordered separately at one thousand two hundred and eighty watt-hours.

The residential storage set contains one hybrid inverter WT-HY5000, one battery, a wall bracket set, one DC cable and one AC cable, plus a communication module with antenna. Backup output must be enabled on the commissioning page; it is switched off by default.

Commercial storage is delivered per project. The cabinet already contains the power conversion system WT-PCS100, the liquid cooling unit and the fire protection module. The AC combiner cabinet and the monitoring gateway needed for parallel operation are optional and confirmed per project list.

## 5 How to Read These Figures

The figures on this page are factory nominal values, measured at twenty-five degrees Celsius ambient temperature, nominal grid voltage and a new battery. Actual output power varies with module orientation, shading, ambient temperature and state of charge, with a tolerance of plus or minus five percent.

The nameplate is authoritative. Where batches of the same model differ, the batch figures on the certificate supplied with the unit apply; if the nameplate differs from this page, follow the nameplate and raise a ticket for confirmation instead of changing device parameters yourself.

For figures not listed here, such as noise, standby consumption or the maximum number of parallel cabinets, consult the installation manual for the specific model or raise a ticket so that after-sales can provide the official data sheet. This document is not a commitment on unlisted performance.
