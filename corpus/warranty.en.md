## 1 Warranty Scope

WOTAI Energy provides a limited warranty for the complete energy storage system described in this document. During the warranty period, any failure caused by defective material or faulty workmanship will be repaired or replaced by WOTAI Energy free of charge.

The warranty covers the battery module, the hybrid inverter, the main control board, the communication module and the accessories supplied with the unit. The following are excluded: unauthorised disassembly or modification, use of non-original spare parts, operation outside the environmental conditions printed on the nameplate, damage caused by water ingress, lightning, fire or animal bites, and damage caused by missing earth bonding or missing surge protection as required by the installation manual.

The warranty period starts on the date the installer completes the grid-connection registration in the service system. If no registration record is available, the period starts ninety days after the manufacturing date.

## 2 Warranty Period

The complete system carries a ten-year warranty. The battery module is guaranteed to retain at least seventy percent of its rated usable capacity for ten years; if capacity falls below that value within six thousand full cycles, WOTAI Energy will replace the module with an equivalent part free of charge.

The hybrid inverter carries a ten-year warranty, while the communication module and the display panel carry a five-year warranty. Replacement parts inherit the remaining warranty of the original system; the ten-year period is not restarted.

Labour for warranty repairs is covered by WOTAI Energy. Travel costs for site visits beyond two hundred kilometres are charged to the requesting party.

## 3 Claim Procedure

End users must file a claim through their installer. The installer creates a ticket in the after-sales system within twenty-four hours of receiving the request. The ticket must include the product serial number, the fault code, the installation date and photographs of the site.

WOTAI Energy completes an initial assessment within one working day and provides remote diagnostics steps. If remote diagnostics cannot resolve the issue, spare parts are dispatched within three working days.

After the parts arrive, the installer performs the replacement and uploads the replacement record. Old parts must be returned according to the recycling instruction issued by WOTAI Energy; parts not returned as instructed are invoiced at market price.

## 4 Exclusions and Language

WOTAI Energy accepts no warranty liability where the owner has changed grid parameters, added a third-party inverter or connected an uncertified expansion battery, but paid repair remains available.

This warranty is governed by the Chinese text. If a translation is ambiguous, the Chinese version prevails and translations are provided for installer reference only.

## 5 Contact and Escalation

If a ticket receives no reply within three working days, the installer can escalate by email or by calling the regional after-sales line. Please quote the original ticket number so that no duplicate ticket is created.

For safety-related symptoms such as smoke, unusual smell, deformed enclosure or abnormal temperature, switch off the grid connection immediately, keep clear of the unit, do not disassemble it, and call the local emergency number as well as the WOTAI Energy after-sales hotline.
